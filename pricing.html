Pricing FAQಬೆಲೆ FAQ

What is not included in the package price? ▾

Third-party costs such as your domain name and hosting, paid advertising, and any content writing beyond the agreed scope are billed separately or paid directly by you.

What happens after the site goes live? ▾

You own the website and all access. We can continue with clearly scoped updates under a separate support agreement, or hand everything over for you to manage.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings or a number of customers? ▾

No. We build a fast, well-structured, correctly tagged site and set up your contact routes. Search rankings and customer numbers depend on many factors outside our control, so we do not promise specific results.

How long does it take? ▾

Most packages are ready for review within 3 to 5 business days of receiving your business details and content. This is an estimate, not a guarantee, and depends on how quickly content and approvals come through.
