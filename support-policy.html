This is a general template reflecting how we intend to operate. It is not legal advice. Please review it against your own business practices and applicable law before relying on it.

Included supportಒಳಗೊಂಡ ಬೆಂಬಲ

For 14 days after handover, we fix at no extra cost any defect in what we built — broken links, layout problems, or contact routes that do not work as agreed. This does not cover new requests or content changes.

Content changesವಿಷಯ ಬದಲಾವಣೆಗಳು

Changes to text, images, prices or adding new sections are treated as updates and quoted separately, or covered under a maintenance agreement.

Refundsಮರುಪಾವತಿಗಳು

The advance paid to begin work is non-refundable once design or development has started, because it covers work already done.

If we are unable to deliver the agreed scope for reasons within our control, we refund the unused portion of the amount paid for work not yet performed.

Third-party costs already paid (domains, hosting, subscriptions) cannot be refunded by us.

What we cannot supportನಾವು ಬೆಂಬಲಿಸಲಾಗದಿರುವುದು

We cannot fix problems caused by changes made by others to the code or hosting after handover, or by third-party platform outages. We can help on a paid basis if you would like.

How to request supportಬೆಂಬಲವನ್ನು ಹೇಗೆ ವಿನಂತಿಸುವುದು

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 94807 27214 with a short description and, where possible, a screenshot. We aim to respond within one working day.
